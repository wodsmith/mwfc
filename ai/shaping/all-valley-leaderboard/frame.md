# All Valley Open 2026 Leaderboards — Frame

## Source

> I have a competition happening on https://wodsmith.com/compete/the-all-valley-open-2026
> We have a planetscale database that I have direct access to to pull data from.
>
> I want to display two leaderboards for this competition. One for the two divisions
> like a normal competition and how the competition leaderboard is set up.
>
> The primary reason I want a page/leaderboard on this site is that we want to build
> a custom leaderboard that combines all the gyms that are participating into a
> leaderboard and aggregating the athletes registered to that gym into a single gym
> score so we can see who is the fittest gym in the valley.

---

## Problem

The All Valley Open 2026 runs 3 CF Open workouts across 2 divisions (Men's/Women's Open, ~68 athletes). The standard leaderboard lives on WODsmith — but there's no way to see **gym-level standings**. Athletes register with an affiliate, and the organizer wants to aggregate those individual scores into a "Fittest Gym in the Valley" ranking. That doesn't exist anywhere today.

## Outcome

- Visitors to the MWFC site see individual division leaderboards for the All Valley Open
- A "Fittest Gym" leaderboard ranks participating gyms by aggregated athlete scores
- Both leaderboards update as scores come in during the competition window (Feb 28 – Mar 14)
